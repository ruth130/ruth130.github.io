About AccessAble Accessibility Consulting

Ruth Kearney, B.A, CIP | Certified Accessibility Consultant | EST. 2025

Professional Expertise

Accessibility means dignity, autonomy, and policy integrity. My approach combines Brock University Certified expertise with a B.A in Psychology and a decade of Chartered Insurance Professional (CIP)-designated experience to ensure your recommendations are legally robust and human-centered.

Lived Experience

My work is rooted in a trauma-informed approach, recognizing that systemic barriers often carry an emotional weight. I prioritize intersectionality, acknowledging how neurodivergence overlaps with other disabilities and identities to create unique lived experiences.

As an autistic professional raising neurodivergent children alongside my ND spouse, I live accessibility every day. I am here to help you bridge the gap between technical code and the lived reality of neurodivergence.
